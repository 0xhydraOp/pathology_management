Recover owner access

Recovery requires your current owner password, a previously verified independent recovery authenticator, and a private one-use maintenance link issued by the authenticated deployment administrator. The link alone does not grant owner access.

The repair session can only replace the primary authenticator. Customer and licence data stay locked until the new primary is verified and prior sessions are revoked. Sign in again afterward. If you lost the password or both authenticators, this flow cannot recover access; no public reset or bypass account is available.
